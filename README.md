# Game with AI: Starter Pack

**Prompts that move: an Opus 5.5 field guide for games, art, animation, 3D and coded video.**

A single-page guide to briefing Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 (with notes for GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol) for animations, games, dioramas, 3D scenes and coded video. It has live demos, starter prompts, a prompt builder and checklists for every craft from art direction to performance.

**Read it online:** https://2600th.github.io/game-with-ai-starter-pack/

Facts checked 6 Oct 2026. Built from Anthropic's model docs, OpenAI's model notes, and the prompts and skills people shipped in the first week after launch.

## What's inside

- **Start here:** pick what you're making, the 60-second version, anatomy of a build brief, one line per visible change
- **Playbooks by format:** games, animation, dioramas, 3D scenes and coded video
- **Engines and libraries:** choosing the stack
- **Design and look:** game design, art direction (reference first), illustration, SVG and generative art
- **Assets:** building, measuring and sourcing 3D assets; generating assets with hosted models using your own keys, or locally with ComfyUI
- **Systems:** level design, game AI, procedural generation, physics and character controllers, camera systems
- **Motion and feel:** character animation, VFX, shaders and post-processing, sound and music from code, game juice
- **Shipping:** UI, HUD and accessibility; coded video frameworks and determinism; performance and profiling
- **Working process:** iterating like a director, multi-session builds, the build contract, effort and long runs
- **Model choice:** Opus 5.5 vs Sonnet 5.5, plus what to add when briefing GPT-6 Astra or GPT-6.1 Sol
- **Tools:** eight prompt shapes, traps and what to write instead, a prompt builder, a pre-send checklist and sources
